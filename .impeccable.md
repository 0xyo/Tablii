## Design Context

### Users
**Normal person and Gen-Z diners** ordering at a restaurant table via QR code scanning. Also **restaurant managers/staff** operating dashboards and interfaces during service. Both groups value efficiency and premium experience.

### Jobs to be Done
- **Customers**: Browse menu, build cart, checkout, track order status, leave reviews — with minimal friction, on-table convenience
- **Staff**: Manage orders, track kitchen progress, handle payments, view analytics — with clarity during high-pressure service
- **Both**: Complete tasks quickly without distraction

### Brand Personality
**Elegant, Effortless, Modern**
- Effortless: Fast, intuitive, no unnecessary steps
- Elegant: Premium without pretension; refined and intentional
- Modern: Contemporary, responsive, thoughtfully designed

### Aesthetic Direction
**Luxury Refined** — not corporate, not trendy, not generic AI slop.

This means:
- Avoid clichés: no Inter/Roboto/system fonts, no purple gradients, no predictable card grids with icons above headings
- Avoid trends: no overused color schemes, no cookie-cutter layouts
- Embrace: Unexpected typography, custom color choices, character-driven design that feels *specifically* designed for Tablii
- Tone: Premium dining experience translated to digital — sophisticated, uncluttered, with breathing room

### Theme
**Both light and dark** with user toggle. Theme choice should reflect context:
- **Dark** (charcoal-900 base): For table-side ordering (reduced eye strain, intimate feel, evening service)
- **Light** (cream/off-white base): For staff dashboards and admin (clarity during bright kitchen/office environments)

### Color Strategy
Avoid predictable choices. Move away from trend colors toward unexpected palette:
- **Keep existing jewel-tone accents** (emerald, sapphire, ruby, topaz) but use *sparingly*
- **Tint neutrals** (charcoal, cream) toward brand hue (amber/warm tone) for subconscious cohesion
- **60-30-10 rule**: 60% neutral/surface, 30% secondary text/borders, 10% accent
- **Accents work because they're rare** — don't overuse

### Design Principles
1. **Intentionality over trends** — Every visual choice should have a reason rooted in user context, not following fashions
2. **Refined simplicity** — Remove unnecessary elements; every detail earns its place
3. **Ergonomic clarity** — Layouts adapt to context (table-side phone vs. desktop dashboard) without sacrificing sophistication
4. **Unexpected choices** — Avoid reflex defaults; choose fonts, colors, and patterns that feel specifically designed for Tablii
5. **Accessibility as craft** — WCAG AA with digital accessibility and ergonomic layouts; premium design includes *everyone*

### Technical Constraints
- **Framework**: Tailwind CSS 3.4 + Jinja2 templates
- **Fonts currently loaded**: Playfair Display (display), DM Sans (body), Cairo (Arabic), JetBrains Mono (mono)
- **RTL support**: Must work seamlessly in French, Arabic, and English
- **Real-time**: WebSocket features for live order updates, notifications
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first; phone at table, tablet in kitchen, desktop in office

### Differentiation / One Thing to Remember
**A premium dining experience that feels *designed* rather than *built*** — intuitive enough that Gen-Z scrolls through it effortlessly, refined enough that managers trust it for their business.
